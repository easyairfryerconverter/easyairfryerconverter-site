Tips for better results

1. Use a food thermometer. Poultry should reach 165°F / 74°C internally.

2. Thickness matters more than weight alone, especially for chicken breast.

3. Leave space around pieces so hot air can circulate.

4. Check a few minutes early the first time you cook a specific cut in your air fryer.
