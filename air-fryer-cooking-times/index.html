5 tips for better air fryer results

1. Check early. Air fryers cook differently. Start checking several minutes before the end of a range.

2. Leave room for airflow. Crowding can slow cooking and reduce crispness.

3. Shake or turn when useful. Fries, nuggets and many vegetables benefit from movement halfway through.

4. Use a thermometer for meat. Time alone cannot confirm safe doneness.

5. Treat this as a starting chart. Thickness, quantity, starting temperature and air fryer model all matter.
