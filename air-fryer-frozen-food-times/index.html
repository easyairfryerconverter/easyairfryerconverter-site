Air Fryer Frozen Food Cooking Times

These are practical starting ranges. Exact cooking time depends on thickness, quantity, starting temperature, and air fryer model.

FoodTemperatureApprox. timeTurn / shakeHelpful note
Thin frozen fries 390°F 12–16 min Shake halfway Avoid overfilling the basket
Thick frozen fries 390°F 12–20 min Shake halfway Add a little time for a fuller basket
Frozen chicken nuggets 390°F 6–10 min Shake halfway Cook until hot throughout
Frozen fish sticks 390°F 6–10 min Turn if needed Best in a single layer
Frozen mozzarella sticks 360°F 8–10 min Shake gently Check early to reduce cheese leakage
Frozen onion rings 390°F 8–12 min Shake halfway Do not overcrowd

Tips for better results

1. Most frozen foods can go directly from freezer to air fryer unless the package says otherwise.

2. Shake or turn halfway for more even browning.

3. A crowded basket usually means longer cooking and less crispness.

4. Package directions take priority when they provide specific air fryer instructions.
