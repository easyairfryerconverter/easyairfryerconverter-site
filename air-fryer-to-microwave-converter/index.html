FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about this tool and air fryer cooking.

Can I convert air fryer time directly to microwave time?

Only as a rough starting estimate. Microwaves heat with moisture-driven energy while air fryers use circulating hot air, so the result depends heavily on the food and the goal.

Will food stay crispy in the microwave?

Usually not. A microwave is excellent for fast reheating, but it does not brown or crisp the surface the way an air fryer can. Breaded foods, fries and pizza will normally have a softer texture.

Should I microwave food all at once or in short intervals?

Short intervals are safer for reheating because microwaves can create hot and cold spots. Check and stir, turn or rotate the food when appropriate, then continue only as needed.

Does microwave wattage affect the result?

Yes. Higher-wattage microwaves usually heat faster. Because microwave power varies widely, treat the result from this tool as a conservative starting estimate and shorten the first interval if your microwave is powerful.

Can I microwave food that was meant to be air fried from frozen?

Sometimes, but the texture may be very different and package safety instructions should take priority. Some frozen foods are specifically designed for oven or air-fryer cooking rather than microwave preparation.

How do I know when reheated food is ready?

It should be hot throughout, not just around the edges. For foods that require a safe internal temperature, use a food thermometer rather than relying only on time.
