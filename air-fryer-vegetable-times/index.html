Air Fryer Vegetable Cooking Times

These are practical starting ranges. Exact cooking time depends on thickness, quantity, starting temperature, and air fryer model.

FoodTemperatureApprox. timeTurn / shakeHelpful note
Broccoli florets 375°F 8–12 min Shake halfway Light oil helps browning
Brussels sprouts, halved 375°F 12–16 min Shake halfway Halve larger sprouts
Potato cubes 360°F 12–18 min Shake halfway Keep pieces similar in size
Potato wedges 360°F 18–22 min Shake halfway Dry before cooking
Asparagus 375°F 6–9 min Turn once Thicker spears may need longer
Zucchini slices 375°F 7–10 min Shake gently Avoid overcrowding

Tips for better results

1. Cut vegetables into similar-sized pieces for more even cooking.

2. A light coating of oil often improves browning and texture.

3. Shake the basket halfway for vegetables with many small pieces.

4. Water-heavy vegetables can soften quickly, so start checking early.
