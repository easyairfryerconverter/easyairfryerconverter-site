Air Fryer Beginner’s Guide

A simple starting guide for first-time air fryer users: setup, preheating, basket space, timing and safety.

Start simple

Begin with foods you already know, such as frozen fries, vegetables or chicken pieces.

Use airflow

Leave some space around food whenever possible so hot air can circulate.

Check early

Air fryer models vary, so start checking before the end of a suggested time range.

Use a thermometer

For meat and poultry, temperature is more reliable than cooking time alone.

Learn your machine

Basket size, wattage and fan strength can all affect cooking speed.
