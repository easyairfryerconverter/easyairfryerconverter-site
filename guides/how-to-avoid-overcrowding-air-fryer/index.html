How to Avoid Overcrowding an Air Fryer

Why basket space matters and what to do when you need to cook a larger amount.

Explore more All beginner guides Cooking times Oven converter Microwave converter

Air needs room

Crowding blocks hot air and can make food cook unevenly.

Single layer is ideal for crisping

Especially for fries, breaded foods and vegetables.

Cook in batches when needed

Two crisp batches often beat one crowded batch.

Expect more time with fuller baskets

If you do cook more at once, shake more often and allow extra time.
