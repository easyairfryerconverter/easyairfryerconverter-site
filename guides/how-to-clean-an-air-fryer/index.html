How to Clean an Air Fryer

A simple routine for keeping the basket, tray and cooking chamber clean without damaging nonstick surfaces.

Explore more All beginner guides Cooking times Oven converter Microwave converter

Unplug and cool first

Never clean the appliance while it is hot or connected to power.

Wash removable parts gently

Use warm soapy water and a non-abrasive sponge unless your manufacturer says dishwasher-safe.

Wipe the interior

Use a damp cloth for crumbs and grease after the unit is cool.

Avoid harsh abrasives

Scratching nonstick surfaces can shorten their life.

Dry before reassembly

Make sure removable parts are dry before putting the unit back together.
