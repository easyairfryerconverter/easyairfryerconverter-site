How to Reheat Food in an Air Fryer

How to reheat pizza, fries, chicken and other leftovers without drying them out.

Explore more All beginner guides Cooking times Oven converter Microwave converter

Use moderate heat

Around 325–350°F / 165–175°C works well for many leftovers.

Check early

Reheating often takes only a few minutes.

Give food space

A single layer helps restore crispness.

Add moisture only when needed

Dry foods may benefit from a tiny amount of oil; saucy foods usually do not.
