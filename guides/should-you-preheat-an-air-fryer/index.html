Should You Preheat an Air Fryer?

When preheating helps, when it does not matter much, and how to decide for your own air fryer.

Explore more All beginner guides Cooking times Oven converter Microwave converter

Follow your model first

If the manufacturer recommends preheating for a recipe, use that guidance.

Crisping can benefit

Short preheating can help foods like fries, breaded items and some meats start browning sooner.

Many foods do fine without it

Frozen snacks and everyday reheating often work well from a cold start.

Keep it short

For many home air fryers, a few minutes is enough when you choose to preheat.
