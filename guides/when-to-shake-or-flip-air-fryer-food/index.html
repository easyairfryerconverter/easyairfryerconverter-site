When to Shake or Flip Air Fryer Food

A practical guide to when shaking the basket or turning food helps it cook more evenly.

Explore more All beginner guides Cooking times Oven converter Microwave converter

Shake small pieces

Fries, nuggets and chopped vegetables often benefit from a shake halfway through.

Flip larger pieces

Chicken breasts, chops and burgers usually cook more evenly when turned.

Do not overdo it

Opening the basket too often can slow cooking.

Use visual cues

If one side is browning faster, turning the food is usually worthwhile.
