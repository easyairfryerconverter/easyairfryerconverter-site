1–5 minutes in the microwave: what does that mean in an air fryer?

Microwave time does not convert with one exact multiplier. The food, starting temperature, microwave wattage and desired texture matter more. The examples below are a practical reference for refrigerated, ready-to-eat leftovers being reheated in an air fryer—not raw meat or poultry.

Microwave directionsAir fryer starting pointUse this for
1 minute350°F for about 4–8 minSmall portions of chilled leftovers; check early
2 minutes350°F for about 5–9 minTypical single portions; turn or stir if practical
3 minutes350°F for about 5–9 minAverage chilled leftovers; begin checking near the low end
5 minutes350°F for about 6–10 minLarger or denser reheating portions; check the center
Important: These examples are for reheating ready-to-eat food. Frozen foods, raw chicken, fish and other uncooked foods need food-specific temperatures and safe-doneness checks. Use the interactive converter for those situations.

Food type matters more than the minute number

Three microwave minutes for pizza should not be treated the same as three microwave minutes for frozen fries or chicken. Air fryers cook with moving hot air, so crispness, thickness and basket loading strongly affect the final time.
