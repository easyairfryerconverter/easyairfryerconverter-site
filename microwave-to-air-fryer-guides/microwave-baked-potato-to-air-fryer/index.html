Microwave Baked Potato to Air Fryer

Use the microwave for speed, then finish the potato in the air fryer for a crisper skin.

Quick links Full converter Conversion chart More food guides

Quick starting point

Microwave: 4–8 minutes in the microwave

Air fryer finish: 6–10 minutes at 390°F / 200°C

Important: This is a practical starting range, not an exact one-to-one conversion. Microwave wattage, food amount, starting temperature and air-fryer model all affect the result.

How to use this method

  1. Use the microwave for fast heating when needed.
  2. Move the food to the air fryer once it is heated through or nearly heated through.
  3. Use the air fryer mainly to improve texture, browning or crispness.
  4. Check early and add time in short increments.

Helpful tips

  • Microwave until mostly tender first.
  • Lightly oil and salt the skin before air frying.
  • Turn once for more even crisping.
