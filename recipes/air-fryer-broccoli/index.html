🥦 EASY AIR FRYER RECIPE

Air Fryer Broccoli

Fast roasted-style broccoli with crisp edges and a tender center.

375°F ⏱️ 8–12 minutes 📱 Mobile-friendly
On this page Ingredients Method Tips Cooking-time chart

Ingredients

  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: garlic powder, lemon, parmesan

How to make it

  1. Cut florets into similar sizes.
  2. Toss lightly with oil and seasoning.
  3. Air fry at 375°F / 190°C for 8–12 minutes.
  4. Shake halfway through.
  5. Finish with lemon or parmesan if desired.

Helpful tips

1. Similar-sized florets cook more evenly.

2. Check small florets early.

3. A little oil helps browning.
