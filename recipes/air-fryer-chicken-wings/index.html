🍗 EASY AIR FRYER RECIPE

Easy Air Fryer Chicken Wings

Simple, crisp air fryer chicken wings with a short ingredient list and an easy method for weeknights or game day.

360°F ⏱️ 18–22 minutes 📱 Mobile-friendly
On this page Ingredients Method Tips Cooking-time chart

Ingredients

  • 1 lb / 450 g chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: garlic powder, paprika, or your favorite wing seasoning

How to make it

  1. Pat the wings dry with paper towel.
  2. Toss with oil and seasoning.
  3. Arrange in the basket with space for airflow.
  4. Air fry at 360°F / 180°C for about 18–22 minutes, turning halfway.
  5. Cook until the thickest part reaches a safe internal temperature, then sauce if desired.
Food safety: Chicken should reach 165°F / 74°C internally.

Helpful tips

1. Dry wings brown better.

2. Avoid packing the basket tightly.

3. For extra crispness, add 2–3 minutes at the end if needed.
