Crispy Air Fryer Frozen Fries

An easy method for crisp frozen fries in the air fryer with no thawing required.

390°F ⏱️ 12–18 minutes 📱 Mobile-friendly
On this page Ingredients Method Tips Cooking-time chart

Ingredients

  • Frozen French fries
  • Optional: a light spray of oil
  • Salt or seasoning after cooking

How to make it

  1. Preheat only if your air fryer model recommends it.
  2. Add frozen fries to the basket without thawing.
  3. Air fry at 390°F / 200°C for 12–18 minutes.
  4. Shake the basket well halfway through.
  5. Season after cooking and serve immediately.

Helpful tips

1. A fuller basket may need more time.

2. Shake more than once for very full baskets.

3. Package air-fryer instructions take priority when provided.
