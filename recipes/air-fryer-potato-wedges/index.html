Crispy Air Fryer Potato Wedges

Simple potato wedges with crisp edges and a fluffy center, made with pantry basics.

360°F ⏱️ 18–22 minutes 📱 Mobile-friendly
On this page Ingredients Method Tips Cooking-time chart

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: paprika, garlic powder, dried herbs

How to make it

  1. Cut potatoes into similar-sized wedges.
  2. Rinse and dry well.
  3. Toss with oil and seasonings.
  4. Air fry at 360°F / 180°C for 18–22 minutes, shaking halfway.
  5. Add a few extra minutes if you want more browning.

Helpful tips

1. Dry potatoes well before cooking.

2. Keep wedge sizes consistent.

3. Do not overcrowd the basket.
