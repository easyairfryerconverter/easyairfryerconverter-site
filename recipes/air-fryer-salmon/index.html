Easy Air Fryer Salmon

A straightforward air fryer salmon method for a quick dinner with minimal cleanup.

360°F ⏱️ 10–15 minutes 📱 Mobile-friendly
On this page Ingredients Method Tips Cooking-time chart

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: lemon, garlic, dill, or paprika

How to make it

  1. Pat salmon dry and season.
  2. Place fillets skin-side down if they have skin.
  3. Air fry at 360°F / 180°C for about 10–15 minutes depending on thickness.
  4. Check early and stop when the center is cooked to your preference and safe doneness.

Helpful tips

1. Thicker fillets need more time.

2. Avoid overcrowding.

3. A thermometer is more reliable than color alone.
